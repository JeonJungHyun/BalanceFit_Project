# Free Trial Policy

## Reservation Requirement

Free trial sessions are available by advance reservation only.

Reservation channels may include:

- Official phone contact
- KakaoTalk channel
- Naver reservation

Trial availability may vary depending on studio schedule, instructor availability, and current promotions.

## Trial Cost

Depending on promotions, a trial session may be offered as:

- Free
- Low-cost trial, typically 10,000 KRW to 20,000 KRW

Members should confirm the current trial cost before booking.

## Registration Benefits

Members who register immediately after a trial session may receive special benefits.

Possible registration benefits include:

- Discounted registration
- Additional sessions
- Special promotional benefits

Promotional benefits may vary by studio, location, and registration period.

## Regional Information

Users can search local Pilates centers through:

- Naver Maps
- Official websites
- Local reservation platforms

Availability may vary by location.
