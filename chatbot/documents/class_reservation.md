# Class Reservation Guide

## Available Class Types

### Private Lessons

Private lessons provide 1:1 personalized Pilates instruction.

Private lessons are recommended for:

- Posture correction
- Rehabilitation
- Individualized coaching

Private lessons are suitable for members who need detailed feedback, customized exercise plans, or focused instructor attention.

### Group Lessons

Group lessons are typically operated as 4:1 to 8:1 classes.

Group lessons are suitable for:

- General fitness
- Cost efficiency
- Social learning environment

Group lessons are a good option for members who want regular exercise at a lower per-session cost.

## Reservation Methods

Reservations may be made through:

- Official contact channels
- KakaoTalk
- Naver Reservation
- Integrated reservation platforms

Members should confirm class availability before visiting the studio.

## Pricing Overview

Private lessons usually cost 60,000 KRW to 80,000 KRW per session.

Group lessons usually cost 20,000 KRW to 30,000 KRW per session.

Pricing may vary depending on lesson type, package count, instructor assignment, and studio policy.
