# Refund and Cancellation Policy

## Refund Standard

Refunds follow the Korean Consumer Dispute Resolution Standards.

Members should review the refund terms before registration because refund amounts may vary depending on the package, usage status, and cancellation timing.

## Mid-Term Cancellation

For mid-term cancellation, the refundable amount is generally calculated by subtracting used sessions and any cancellation fee from the total payment amount.

Refund Amount =
Total Payment
- Used Sessions
- Cancellation Fee

## Cancellation Fee

The cancellation fee may be up to 10% of the total payment amount.

The exact cancellation fee may depend on the studio policy and the applicable consumer dispute resolution standards.

## Multi-Session Package Notice

Promotional packages and discounted packages may use the standard session rate when calculating refunds.

This can significantly reduce the final refundable amount.

Members are encouraged to review refund conditions before registration.
