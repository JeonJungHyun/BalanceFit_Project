# Pilates Pricing Policy

## General Pricing Levels

### Private Lessons (1:1)

Private Pilates lessons usually cost about 60,000 KRW to 80,000 KRW per session.

Multi-session packages may reduce the per-session cost.

### Group Lessons

Group Pilates lessons usually cost about 20,000 KRW to 30,000 KRW per session.

Group lessons are usually operated with 4 to 8 participants.

### Monthly Membership

A monthly Pilates membership typically ranges from 250,000 KRW to 350,000 KRW.

This estimate is based on two sessions per week.

## Pricing Factors

### Lesson Type

Pricing may vary based on the selected lesson type and service conditions.

Common pricing factors include:

- Private lesson
- Group lesson
- Equipment usage
- Instructor assignment

### Package Count

Pilates studios may offer packages with different session counts.

Common examples include:

- 10 sessions
- 20 sessions
- 30 sessions

Higher package counts generally result in lower per-session pricing.

### Validity Period

Package validity periods may affect overall pricing efficiency.

Longer validity periods generally provide better pricing efficiency because members have more time to use their sessions.
