# General Inquiries

## Studio Information

Contact the studio directly for:

- Operating hours
- Instructor schedules
- Class availability
- Membership information

Studio information may vary by location, date, and current reservation status.

## Lesson Recommendations

Private lessons are generally recommended for:

- Rehabilitation
- Posture correction
- Chronic pain management

Group lessons are generally recommended for:

- Beginners
- Regular exercise
- Budget-conscious members

Members who are unsure which lesson type to choose should contact the studio for guidance based on their goals and physical condition.

## Additional Assistance

If information cannot be found in the FAQ or reservation guide, members should contact the studio directly.

Studio staff can provide the most accurate information about current schedules, pricing, promotions, and reservation availability.
